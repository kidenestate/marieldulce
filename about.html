Freshly made with heart

At Mariel Dulce Cakes, every cake begins with a simple question: what kind of moment are we celebrating? From birthdays to small family gatherings and meaningful milestones, we create cakes that feel personal, beautiful and delicious.

We focus on thoughtful design, flavour choices and careful finishing so each cake feels just right for the people who will enjoy it. Whether it is a classic vanilla treat or a custom celebration cake, we believe the details matter.

  • Custom cakes for birthdays, weddings and personal celebrations
  • Elegant finishing touches that match your theme or colours
  • Friendly support to help you plan a cake that fits your occasion

Our process

How we work

We like to make the process simple and enjoyable. You tell us what you are celebrating, we suggest a flavour or design direction, and together we shape the perfect cake for your day.

We can work with your preferred colour palette, occasion theme, message and delivery needs so the cake feels personal from the very start.

Need a custom design?

Share your inspiration, colours, size and date. We will help you plan something that looks beautiful and tastes just as lovely.
